NEPALMC / GAME MODE

Semi-Vanilla

A familiar Minecraft survival world, with a few thoughtful custom features to keep the experience fresh.

Seasonal world · Resets every few months based on activity and how the community plays

A world shaped by players

Vanilla at heart.

Semi-Vanilla is designed to feel close to a regular Minecraft world, with a small set of custom systems layered in. There is no central spawn and no warp network. Travel, exploration, and the places players build are part of the experience.

Shards are earned through playtime, crate rewards, and occasional staff drops. In PvP, the default loss is 65% of a player's Shards; ranks reduce that share.

Server rules

1. Hacking & Unfair Advantages

  • No hacked clients or cheats that provide an unfair advantage.
  • No X-ray mods, resource packs or texture packs.
  • No Freecam or camera exploits to scout bases or gain an unfair advantage.
  • No KillAura, Aimbot, Reach, Fly, Speed or other unfair modifications.
  • No autoclickers or macros that provide an unfair advantage.
  • No exploiting glitches to gain unfair advantages.

2. Griefing & Raiding

  • Griefing is allowed. There is no claim or land-protection system on this server.
  • Players are free to raid, loot, steal and destroy other players' builds.
  • Protect your valuables and builds at your own risk.
  • Staff will not restore items or buildings lost through legitimate raiding or griefing.

3. Chat Rules

  • No spamming or flooding the chat.
  • No excessive use of capital letters.
  • No advertising other Minecraft servers.
  • No harassment, bullying or targeted hate.
  • No racism, discrimination or hateful language.
  • Respect other players and keep conversations reasonable.
  • No impersonating staff members.

4. Exploits & Abuse

  • No item duplication glitches or exploits.
  • No abusing bugs for personal gain.
  • No intentionally causing server lag or disrupting gameplay.
  • Report major bugs and exploits to staff.

5. Real-Money Trading

  • No IRL trading (buying or selling in-game items, accounts, currency or services for real-world money).
  • All real-money purchases must go through official NepalMC store channels.

6. General Rules

  • Respect staff members and their decisions.
  • No ban or punishment evasion using alternate accounts.
  • Do not intentionally disrupt server operations.
  • Follow these rules and use common sense.

If you do not follow these rules, you must accept the punishment given by any staff member. The punishment will depend on the severity and extent of the violation.
SEMI-VANILLA

Features

01

Player-led exploration

No server spawn or warps. Set out on foot, find your own corner of the world, and make the journey part of the adventure.

02

Seasonal world

The world starts fresh every few months. Reset timing follows server activity and community play habits, so each season can grow at its own pace.

03

Custom-built shop

A custom shop uses limited stock. Items can run out, so check what is available in game.

04

Earn Shards through play

Shards are the server's special currency. Players can earn them through playtime rewards, crate rewards, and occasional drops from senior staff.

05

PvP Shard stakes

When a player is killed in PvP, a share of their Shards goes to the attacker. Default players risk 65%; rank perks reduce the percentage lost.
