Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. This site also may use similar technologies such as localStorage or sessionStorage.

2. Technologies Used

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. It stores one tab-scoped preference so the cookie notice stays dismissed while you browse between pages in that tab:

  • nepalmc-cookie-notice-dismissed in sessionStorage — whether you dismissed the cookie notice in the current tab. It is cleared when the tab session ends.

If third‑party services (e.g., analytics) are added in the future, the policy will be updated accordingly.

3. Managing Cookies

The notice explains the essential browser storage used by this site. Closing the tab ends that session preference, so the notice appears again the next time you open the site in a new tab.
